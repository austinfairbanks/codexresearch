# Laboratory sourcing and price snapshots

## Procurement standard

For legitimate laboratory research, sourcing should run through institutional procurement and an approved protocol. A defensible lot record includes supplier identity, chain of custody, batch number, and lot-specific identity and purity evidence such as LC-MS and analytical HPLC. Depending on the experiment, residual solvents, water content, counter-ion, endotoxin, bioburden, sterility, and stability may also matter. A generic certificate, website purity claim, or “research use only” label is not independent verification. Price does not establish quality.
